The Vision

Empowering Civil Administration with Sovereign, Responsible AI

YASHADA’s excellence in administrative training combined with PKC’s deep-tech translation ecosystem creates Maharashtra’s benchmark model for civil officer AI literacy, localized automation, and empirical governance research.

Four Strategic Pillars of Collaboration

01

Executive Capacity Building

Tiered, non-technical executive modules for IAS, Collectors, and HODs on AI decision-making, ethics, data privacy, and procurement.

02

Voice & Vernacular AI

Speech-to-Speech & Bhashini integrations enabling rural citizens to interact in spoken Marathi for entitlements & grievances.

03

Case Studies & Research

Co-authoring empirical case studies of digital interventions across Maharashtra districts to establish policy benchmarks.

04

Gov-Tech Sandbox

A dedicated pilot testbed for administrative officers to test rapid Proof-of-Concepts (PoCs) on on-premise secure infrastructure.
